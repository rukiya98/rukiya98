## 🧠 About

Data scientist who kept drifting toward the engineering side of the problem — and stayed there.

Most of my work sits at the seam between **models and the people who have to use them**: agentic LLM systems that do real work, enterprise BI models that survive contact with messy multi-company ERP data, and real-time browser experiments that put computer vision in front of a non-technical audience.

```yaml
role:        Data Scientist → AI / Forward Deployed Engineer
focus:       Agentic systems · LLM tooling · Applied ML · Decision intelligence
domain:      Enterprise ERP · Business Intelligence · Quant / Algo Trading
currently:   Building "Tropical AI" — an internal AI cockpit for operations
studying:    BSc (Hons) Information Technology — UCSC, Univ. of Colombo
philosophy:  ship it, watch someone use it, fix what actually broke
```

`MediaPipe` `Three.js` `WebGL`
